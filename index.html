About Temple

Shri Khatu Shyam Mandir is one of the most peaceful devotional places where thousands of devotees visit to seek Baba Shyam's blessings.

Baba Shyam is worshipped as the Kaliyug Avatar of Barbarik. Every devotee visiting the temple experiences peace, positivity and divine grace.
